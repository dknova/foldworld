Colophon.

Foldworld is a carousel book: six pages tied back to back so they open into a star of little rooms. Each room is a chapter of Odile Penhallow’s work as a paper engineer.

Everything you see is paper: folded, scored and stood up in three.js. The paper, the props and Odile herself were drawn for this book, then cut out and given thickness in code.

Set in Pixelify Sans and Gaegu. Odile, her studio and Foldworld are fictional, made up for this book.

Write to hello@foldworld.studio. She replies on paper.
